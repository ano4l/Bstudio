Before the studio

Prepare for your finish.

A little preparation creates a calmer appointment and a more refined result.

What to bring

For services excluding hair, bring the requested customised frontal and bundles. For Installation + Curling, Bob Installation and Half Up Half Down, arrive with wig lines already done.

Hair readiness

For either frontal ponytail service, arrive with natural hair relaxed or blown out, with no oils or product. Always check the selected service detail before travelling.

Timing and policies

Appointment policy: A 50% deposit secures every appointment. There is a 15-minute grace period; after 15 minutes the appointment is cancelled. The Lagos Hairline Frontal Ponytail deposit is non-refundable because the HD frontal is purchased and customised for you. For other missed appointments, 20% of the total deposit is refunded. Booking is by appointment only: no walk-ins and no guests or extra people.

Studio hours: Monday–Friday 10:00–19:00; Saturday 10:00–16:00; Sunday closed.
